# Field recording sources

Focus FM uses these field recordings and ambience sources:

- `summer-rain.mp3` — [Rain Light.wav by Ambientsoundapp](https://freesound.org/s/536223/)
- `slow-waves.mp3` — [Ocean Waves.wav by Noted451](https://freesound.org/s/531015/)
- `open-window.mp3` — [Wind in trees 1.mp3 by alppdcjr12](https://freesound.org/s/662575/)
- `cafe-murmur.mp3` — [cafe ambience by mikemoons](https://freesound.org/s/579500/)
- `soft-fire.mp3` — [Fireplace Fire Crackling by RyanKingArt](https://freesound.org/s/717579/)
- `window-rain.mp3` — [Rain on window by Nickisawsome74](https://freesound.org/s/669621/)
- `roof-rain.mp3` — [Heavy Rain tin roof.wav by dnlburnett](https://freesound.org/s/336273/)
- `forest-morning.mp3` — [Forest atmosphere with birds in the background by Cinetony](https://freesound.org/s/575381/)
- `flowing-stream.mp3` — [River ambience from MixSoundNow](https://mixsoundnow.com/mp3/river-1m.mp3)
- `night-insects.mp3` — [Cricket ambience from MixSoundNow](https://mixsoundnow.com/mp3/cricket-25s.mp3)
- `quiet-library.mp3` — [Library ambience from MixSoundNow](https://mixsoundnow.com/mp3/library-5m.mp3)
- `quiet-office.mp3` — [Office ambience from MixSoundNow](https://mixsoundnow.com/mp3/office-1m.mp3)
- `train-carriage.mp3` — [Train ambience from MixSoundNow](https://mixsoundnow.com/mp3/train-1m.mp3)
- `airplane-cabin.mp3` — [Clean airplane cabin ambience from SoundDino](https://sounddino.com/en/effects/airplane/)
- `city-window.mp3` — [City ambience from MixSoundNow](https://mixsoundnow.com/mp3/city-1m.mp3)
- `distant-thunder.mp3` — [Quiet distant lightning from SoundDino](https://sounddino.com/en/effects/lightning/)
- `gull-calls.mp3` — [Isolated gull calls from SoundDino](https://sounddino.com/en/effects/seagulls/)

The browser-ready continuous files are normalized to -20 LUFS, encoded as 112
kbps MP3, and rebuilt with a two-second boundary crossfade for smoother
looping. Thunder and gulls use varied arrangements of isolated recordings, with
events roughly every 8–15 seconds, so they remain active, independent layers
without carrying rain or surf.

## Rhythm assets

The repository also includes thirteen complete Hydrogen drum-kit archives,
fifty browser-ready one-shots, and nine authored drum loops. Their original
downloads, credits, and processing notes are recorded in
`assets/drum-packs/SOURCES.md` and `assets/drum-loops/SOURCES.md`.
